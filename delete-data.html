Rabbit Universe – Delete Your Data

Rabbit Universe is developed by VinceGames.

You can delete the data linked to your Rabbit Universe player at any time. Here's how.

Option 1: in the game (instant)

Open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately, and the game data on your phone is wiped.

Option 2: by email

  1. Send an email to vbedapple@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Rabbit Universe - Data deletion request.
  3. Include your in-game nickname and, if possible, your country and approximate leaderboard rank, so we can find your player.
  4. We'll confirm by email once your data has been deleted, within 30 days.

To also remove the game data stored on your phone, simply uninstall Rabbit Universe (or clear its app data in your phone's settings).

What gets deleted

  • Your player ID and nickname
  • Your leaderboard entry (score, country, approximate location, BigBang count and cosmetics)
  • Your guild membership and guild contest progress
  • Your push notification token and notification settings
  • Your sign-in account

What may be kept

  • Purchase records are handled and kept by Google Play / the Apple App Store under their own policies. We don't store your payment details.
  • Advertising data collected by our ad partners (through Unity LevelPlay) is kept under those partners' own privacy policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • If you created a guild that still has other members, the guild itself stays, but your name and membership are removed from it.

Apart from the above, we don't keep any of your data after deletion.

Questions

Contact us at vbedapple@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
