Rabbit Universe – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Rabbit Universe (Android and iOS) collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. Rabbit Universe is developed by VinceGames, an independent solo developer (Vincent Bédard) based in Sherbrooke, Québec, Canada.

Contact: vbedapple@gmail.com — for any privacy question or request.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, postal address, age or gender to play. We collect:

Player ID – an anonymous ID created automatically when you first launch the gameTo identify your player for the leaderboard, guilds, cloud features and fraud prevention

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and its online features (leaderboard, guilds, contests, cloud services).
  • To show optional rewarded ads and deliver in-app purchases.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and to review player reports.
  • To fix bugs and improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share information with

We use the following service providers, which process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies – Unity Gaming Services (anonymous authentication, leaderboards, Cloud Code) and Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads (advertising and ad mediation). Unity privacy policy
  • Google – Firebase (authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging for notifications, analytics) and Google Play (downloads and payments). Google privacy policy
  • Apple – App Store (downloads and payments) and Apple Push Notification service. Apple privacy policy
  • ipwho.is – IP-based country/approximate location lookup (performed once per install).
  • Other players – your nickname, country flag, approximate location on the globe, game statistics, cosmetics and guild membership are visible to other players.

Our ad partners may use device and advertising identifiers to show ads. On iOS, you are asked for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Where data is stored

Data is sent using encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on servers operated by Unity Technologies and Google, which may be located outside Canada (for example in the United States).

5. How long we keep data

We keep your player data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your player data is deleted from our systems (see below). Our partners keep data according to their own policies.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the game: open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your player account, nickname, leaderboard entry, guild membership, contest progress and notification token, and wipes the game data on your device.
  • By email: write to vbedapple@gmail.com with your nickname. See also our data deletion page.

Purchase records are kept by Google Play / Apple under their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Québec's Law 25, Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Email us at vbedapple@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also file a complaint with your local privacy authority (in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information).

8. Children

Rabbit Universe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Community safety

Nicknames and guild names are filtered for offensive words. You can report and block another player by tapping their row on the leaderboard. Reports are reviewed by the developer.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the game or on this page.
